About

From task-specific systems to general spoken language models

The field of speech processing is undergoing a paradigm shift from task-specific systems toward general spoken language models (SLMs) capable of performing diverse speech tasks through natural language instructions or direct conversation. New modeling paradigms, full-duplex interaction and real-world applications have redefined the scope of modern speech systems, while learning under low-resource conditions and avoiding catastrophic forgetting have become central challenges.

This tutorial presents a structured overview of the core design principles, recent advances and open challenges of general spoken language models. It covers how speech understanding, generation and reasoning can be integrated within unified systems, and includes two hands-on sessions: training SLMs while mitigating catastrophic forgetting, and editing auditory knowledge in SLMs without retraining.
